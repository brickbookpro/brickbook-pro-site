Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

Brickbook Pro is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All your project data, site plans, and measurements are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Camera & Storage Permissions

The app requests access to your Camera and Storage solely to allow you to load blueprints and save project data. These files remain on your device unless you choose to manually export or share them.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use Google Firebase/Crashlytics for anonymous stability monitoring to help us fix bugs. This data is not linked to your identity.

4. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, contact us at brickbookpro@gmail.com.
