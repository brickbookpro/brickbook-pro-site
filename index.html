Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you.

1. Data Privacy

TallyTrowel does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All project data, site plans, and measurements are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any external servers.

2. Permissions

The app requests access to your Camera and Storage solely to allow you to load blueprints and save project data. These files remain private to your device.

3. Monitoring

We use Google Firebase/Crashlytics for anonymous stability monitoring. This data is not linked to your identity and is used only to improve app performance.
