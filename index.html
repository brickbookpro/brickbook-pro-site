Built by a Bricklayer

Why I Built TallyTrowel

I built TallyTrowel because I wanted a quicker and easier way to work out masonry quantities from building plans.

Measuring walls, counting bricks and blocks, allowing for openings and then checking everything again takes time — and sometimes you're left wondering whether you've actually counted everything correctly.

I wanted something that could make that process quicker, clearer and easier to check without making it complicated. So I started building TallyTrowel.

Built by a bricklayer, for the people who actually do the work.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

What is TallyTrowel?

TallyTrowel is a growing range of practical software for bricklayers and contractors. The main Android app focuses on masonry takeoff, with separate tools for payments, site records, booking-in and future calculators and desktop takeoffs.

Can I download all the apps now?

No. The Wages Portal & Files is live for existing authorised users. Site Diary and BrickBook Pro are entering live trials. The Android takeoff app, calculators and Windows software have the availability shown in Our Apps. We will add public download links when each product is ready.

Does my portal login work with every app?

No shared login across the range is currently promised. The portal uses its own authorised accounts. The Android takeoff app is designed for local project use without a TallyTrowel account.

Who is TallyTrowel designed for?

It is designed primarily for bricklayers, contractors, estimators, quantity surveyors and housebuilders who need to work out masonry quantities from plans.

Can TallyTrowel calculate cavity walls?

Yes. V1 supports Traditional Cavity construction, including separate brick and block leaves and party-wall connections.

Can I deduct windows and doors?

Yes. Add windows, doors and other openings and TallyTrowel deducts their measured areas from the relevant wall calculations.

Can TallyTrowel calculate pikes?

Yes. TallyTrowel includes a pike calculator for gable areas using pitch and base measurements.

Where are my projects stored?

The Android takeoff app uses local-first project storage with manual backup and restore. The Wages Portal & Files uses online account-based storage. Check the guidance and privacy information for the specific product you use.

Can I create a PDF report?

Yes. Completed takeoffs can be turned into professional PDF reports that can be saved or shared.

What devices is TallyTrowel available on?

TallyTrowel is being developed for Android. Availability and download details will be provided when the release is ready.
